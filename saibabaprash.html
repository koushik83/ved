Frequently Asked Questions About Sai Baba's Answers

How does Sai Baba give answers?

Through divine numbers between 1-720, each containing specific guidance from Sai Baba. Devotees use this traditional method to seek guidance from Saibaba.

Are Sai Baba's answers accurate?

Devotees read these answers with faith, and many find guidance and comfort in them. They are part of a devotional practice and are not a guarantee of any outcome. For important decisions, also use your own judgment.

What questions can I ask Sai Baba?

You can ask about career decisions, relationships, business matters, life problems, and yes/no questions. Sai Baba gives answers for all aspects of life.

About Sai Baba of Shirdi

Shirdi Sai Baba remains a popular saint and is worshipped mainly in Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka and worldwide. Known for answering devotees' questions and giving divine guidance, He is revered by both Hindu and Muslim devotees. His teachings emphasized moral code of love, forgiveness, helping others, charity, contentment, inner peace, and devotion to God and guru.

Sri Sai Baba left his physical body in October 15, 1918, but he is believed to be with us even more now than he was earlier...

Assurances made by Sai Baba to his devotees:

  • Whosoever puts their feet on Shirdi soil, their sufferings will come to an end.
  • The wretched and miserable will rise to joy and happiness as soon as they climb the steps of my Samadhi.
  • I shall be ever active and vigorous even after leaving this earthly body.
  • My tomb shall bless and speak to the needs of my devotees.
  • I shall be active and vigorous even from my tomb.
  • My mortal remains will speak from my tomb.
  • I am ever living to help and guide all who come to me, who surrender to me and who seek refuge in me.
  • If you look to me, I look to you.
  • If you cast your burden on me, I shall surely bear it.
  • If you seek my advice and help, it shall be given to you at once.
  • There shall be no want in the house of my devotee.
