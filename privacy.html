Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary

Ved Prashnavali (vedprashnavali.com) is a free website. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your question is never typed into the site, so it is never collected.

Like most websites, this site uses services from Google to count visits and to show advertising. These services use cookies. The sections below explain what that means and what choices you have.

Information Collected When You Visit

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects information such as the pages you view, the approximate country and city you visit from, your device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site.

It also records actions on the site: which prashnavali was used, which answer number was shown, how many days in a row Today's Guidance was used, use of the share buttons, and installing or opening the app. It does not record your question.

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell visitors apart. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Google Translate

Some pages include the Google Translate tool so that you can read them in another language. When such a page loads, your browser connects to Google to load the tool.

Hosting

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • The Today's Guidance page saves today's answer and your count of days in your browser's local storage. This is kept on your device. We receive only the usage statistics described above.
  • If you install the app, your browser keeps copies of some pages on your device so that they open faster and can work offline.

You can remove both at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Sharing Buttons

The WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram buttons do nothing until you press them. When you press one, the page opens that app or site so that you can share your answer. The Instagram button also copies the answer to your clipboard.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising and of Google Analytics using the links above.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your information.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

For any question about this policy, write to vedicprashnavali@gmail.com or see the Contact page.
