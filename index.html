What Is a Prashnavali?

A prashnavali (प्रश्नावली) is a traditional Hindu way of seeking guidance on a question. The seeker prays, holds one question in mind, and chooses a letter or a number from a sacred chart without looking for a particular one. The chart leads to a verse or a saying, and the devotee reads it as the answer. The best known is the Ram Shalaka Prashnavali, built from nine chaupais of Shri Ramcharitmanas by Goswami Tulsidas and printed in many editions of the book. Other prashnavalis follow the same idea with other deities and texts.

Prashnavali Online with Answers on This Site

  • Ram Shalaka Prashnavali: a chart of 225 letters. The letter you choose leads to one of nine chaupais of Ramcharitmanas, shown with its source, its meaning and whether the answer is favourable.
  • Hanuman Prashnavali: a chakra of 49 numbers from Hanuman Jyotish. Each number has a verse and a message with a clear yes or no.

How to Ask a Prashnavali

  1. Sit quietly, pray to the deity, and keep one clear question in mind.
  2. Ask about one matter at a time, in a form that a yes, a no or a short guidance can answer.
  3. Choose a letter or a number as you feel drawn, without searching for a particular one.
  4. Read the answer with faith and reflect on it. Do not ask the same question again on the same day.

Which Prashnavali Is Most Powerful?

No prashnavali is more powerful than another. Devotees traditionally use the prashnavali of their ishta deva, the deity they worship most. The Ram Shalaka Prashnavali is the oldest and most widely used, because it comes from Ramcharitmanas itself. The Hanuman Prashnavali is preferred when a plain yes or no is wanted. Devotees of Shirdi Sai Baba use the Sai Baba Prashnavali. Whichever you choose, the sincerity of the question matters more than the chart.

Rules of Prashnavali

  • Ask with faith, not for entertainment.
  • One question at a time, and no more than three questions in a day.
  • Do not repeat a question because you did not like the answer.
  • Treat the answer as guidance for reflection. For important decisions, also use your own judgment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the prashnavali free to use online?

Yes. Every prashnavali on this site is free, with no sign-up and no login.

Can I use the prashnavali in Hindi?

Yes. The Ram Shalaka Prashnavali and the Hanuman Prashnavali have full Hindi pages, the chaupais and verses are in Hindi everywhere, and every page has a translate button.

Does the prashnavali give yes or no answers?

The Hanuman Prashnavali gives a clear yes or no with each message. The Ram Shalaka Prashnavali tells you whether the matter is favourable, and the Sai Baba Prashnavali gives a short guidance.

Which is the original prashnavali?

The Ram Shalaka Prashnavali is the original one in print. It appears in editions of Shri Ramcharitmanas, and its nine chaupais are taken from the text.

Are prashnavali answers true?

A prashnavali is a devotional practice. Devotees read the answers with faith and find guidance in them. They are not a guarantee of any outcome.

How many times a day can I ask?

Ask each question once. The traditional guidance is no more than three questions in a day.
