Muhurat times in Bengaluru through the year

In 2027, Brahma Muhurta in Bengaluru begins as early as 04:23 AM (20 May) and as late as 05:07 AM (26 January). Abhijit, the muhurta around local noon, begins between 11:40 AM (23 October) and 12:11 PM (8 February).

What these times are

The good times of the day in Bengaluru, from Bengaluru's own sunrise and sunset: Brahma Muhurta before dawn, Abhijit around midday, and the Amrit, Shubh and Labh choghadiya, leaving out any that fall in Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda or Gulika. Every time is on Bengaluru's clock, daylight saving included.

  • Brahma Muhurta: the second-last muhurta of the night, ending one muhurta before sunrise; the time tradition keeps for prayer, meditation and study.
  • Abhijit Muhurat: the eighth of the day's fifteen muhurtas, around local noon, held good for beginning important work. It is not used on Wednesdays, when that muhurta is a Dur Muhurta.
  • Choghadiya: the day and the night, each divided into eight parts. Amrit, Shubh and Labh are good, Chal (also called Char) is neutral and traditionally used for travel, and Udveg, Kaal and Rog are avoided for beginnings.
  • Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda and Gulika: eighths of the day, a different one on each weekday, that tradition avoids for starting something new.

Weddings, griha pravesh and other ceremonies

This page gives the day's general good times. A muhurat for a wedding, a griha pravesh, a mundan or a new vehicle is chosen by many more rules, often with the family's own details, and is not given here; families usually ask their own priest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shubh muhurat today in Bengaluru?

The list at the top of this page: today's Brahma Muhurta, Abhijit Muhurat and the Amrit, Shubh and Labh choghadiya for Bengaluru, in local time, leaving out any choghadiya that falls in Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda or Gulika. It changes every day with Bengaluru's sunrise and sunset.

Is Abhijit Muhurat used on Wednesday?

No. On a Wednesday the eighth muhurta of the day, the one that is Abhijit on other days, is a Dur Muhurta, so panchangs give no Abhijit that day.

When is Brahma Muhurta tomorrow in Bengaluru?

Press Tomorrow at the top of the page, or see the week's table. Brahma Muhurta ends one muhurta before Bengaluru's sunrise, so it moves a little every day with the sunrise.

Which choghadiya is good for travel?

Chal, also called Char, the one tradition names for travel. Amrit, Shubh and Labh are good for most work; Udveg, Kaal and Rog are avoided for beginnings.

Can I begin something in Rahu Kaal if the choghadiya is good?

Many families wait for Rahu Kaal to pass even then, and this page leaves such a choghadiya out of the good times. The choghadiya tables still show it.

Does daylight saving change the muhurat times in Bengaluru?

Bengaluru keeps the same clock all year, so its muhurat times move only with the seasons.
