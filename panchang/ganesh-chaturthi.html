Ganesh Chaturthi is kept on the Chaturthi of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, with the installation and worship of Ganesha at madhyahna, the middle part of the day. The day is the one whose madhyahna Chaturthi holds, or holds more of where it touches two. The festival closes with the visarjan on Anant Chaturdashi, ten days later.

The table gives, for each city, the day of Ganesh Chaturthi, the part of madhyahna in which Chaturthi is still in force, and the day of Anant Chaturdashi. Many calendars print the whole madhyahna, which can run past the end of Chaturthi. All times are local, with daylight saving allowed for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Ganesh Chaturthi on a different date in New Jersey than in India?

The puja belongs to the middle of the day, and midday comes to each city at a different point of the Chaturthi tithi. In 2027 cities in North America keep it a day before India.

Why should the moon not be seen on Ganesh Chaturthi?

By tradition, seeing the moon that night brings a false accusation (mithya dosha). The moonrise for each night is on the city's page.

When is the visarjan?

On Anant Chaturdashi, the Chaturdashi of the same fortnight. Families who keep Ganesha for one and a half, three, five or seven days immerse him earlier.
