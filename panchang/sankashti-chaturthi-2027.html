Sankashti Chaturthi 2027: dates and moonrise in your city

Sankashti Chaturthi is the Chaturthi of each dark fortnight, kept with a fast that ends at moonrise. The day is the one on which Chaturthi is in force at moonrise, so both the date and the time to break the fast depend on where you are. A Sankashti that falls on a Tuesday is Angarki, the most observed of the year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my city's Sankashti date sometimes differ from India's?

Because the deciding moment is the local moonrise. The tithi is the same stretch of time everywhere; the moon rises at a different point in it in each city.

What is Angarki Sankashti?

A Sankashti Chaturthi that falls on a Tuesday, Mangalavara, the day of Angaraka (Mars). It is held to carry the merit of a year of Sankashti fasts.
