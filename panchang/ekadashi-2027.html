Ekadashi 2027: dates and parana time in your city

Ekadashi is kept twice a lunar month, and the fast is broken the next morning, in Dwadashi, after sunrise and after the first quarter of the Dwadashi tithi (Hari Vasara) has passed. The fast day is the one on which Ekadashi is in force at sunrise; when Dwadashi would be over before the next sunrise, the fast moves a day earlier so that it can be broken in Dwadashi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Ekadashi fall a day apart in different cities?

The tithi is in force at the same instant everywhere, but sunrise is not. In a city where Ekadashi has not begun by sunrise, the fast is the next day.

What is parana?

Breaking the fast. It is done in Dwadashi, after sunrise and after Hari Vasara, and before Dwadashi ends.
