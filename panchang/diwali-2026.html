Diwali 2026: date and Lakshmi Puja time in your city

Lakshmi Puja is performed on the Amavasya of Kartika during pradosh kaal, the first three muhurtas after sunset. The day is the one on which Amavasya is in force in that evening window. Amavasya begins and ends at the same instant everywhere, but sunset is local, so the date can shift by a day between continents.

The table gives, for each city, the day of Lakshmi Puja and the puja window: the part of that evening's pradosh kaal during which Amavasya is in force. All times are local, with daylight saving allowed for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Diwali on a different date in New Jersey than in India?

The puja belongs to the evening, and the evening comes to each city at a different point in the Amavasya tithi. When the tithi begins after sunset in one city and before it in another, the date differs.

Is Diwali the same day as Dhanteras and Govardhan Puja everywhere?

No. Dhanteras is two days before and Govardhan Puja the day after, but each is decided by its own tithi and time of day, so the gaps can stretch or shrink by a day in some cities. The city's own page lists all five days of the festival.

What if the puja window is short?

The window is where the tradition's rule and the sky agree. A short window is still the right one; where it is very short, families usually begin at its start.
