Frequently Asked Questions

Which tithi is shown for each day?

The one in force at sunrise in Surrey, with the time it ends. The tithi named after it begins at that time and runs until the next sunrise or beyond.

Why do the dates of fasts here differ from an Indian calendar?

Each is worked out for Surrey's own sunrise, sunset and moonrise. A tithi runs for the same stretch of time everywhere, but the local day falls at a different point in it.
