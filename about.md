# About Ved Prashnavali | Ram Shalaka, Hanuman and Sai Baba Prashnavali Online

Source: https://vedprashnavali.com/about

## What This Site Offers

Ved Prashnavali is a free website for devotees who use a prashnavali to seek guidance. A prashnavali is a traditional question and answer practice. You hold a question in mind, pray, make a choice on a chart, and read the answer it leads to.

The site brings three of these practices online:

[Today's Guidance](https://vedprashnavali.com/today) tells you how your day is going to be, with new guidance from Shri Ram and Hanuman Ji every day. The [Naam Jaap Counter](https://vedprashnavali.com/jaap) is a digital mala of 108 for counting your jaap.

## How to Use It

Your question stays with you. It is never typed into the site.

## Please Keep in Mind

The answers on this site are part of a devotional practice. They are offered for reflection and faith, and they are not professional advice. For matters of health, law, money or safety, please use your own judgment and consult a qualified professional.

The site is free to use. It may show advertising to cover its running costs.
