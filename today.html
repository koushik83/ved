How Today's Guidance Works

Ask About Your Day gives you one answer each day. When you choose Shri Ram or Hanuman Ji, the page picks one answer by chance from the same answers used in the Ram Shalaka Prashnavali and the Hanuman Prashnavali on this site. The answer stays on this page until midnight, and a new one is ready the next day.

Chaupai of the Day shows one of the nine chaupais of the Ram Shalaka Prashnavali from Shri Ramcharitmanas. It changes at midnight, and everyone sees the same chaupai on the same date.

Your answer and your count of days are saved in this browser, on your device. Your question is never typed in or sent anywhere. The Privacy Policy explains the usage statistics the site collects.

This is a devotional practice. For important decisions, use your own judgment and seek qualified advice.
