# Today's Guidance from Shri Ram and Hanuman Ji | How Will Your Day Be?

Source: https://vedprashnavali.com/today

## How Will Your Day Be?

This guidance stays the same until midnight. Come back tomorrow for new guidance.

## What Today's Guidance Is

Today's Guidance is one reading for the day, drawn from the same texts as the site's prashnavalis. Choose Shri Ram and the reading is one of the nine chaupais of the Ram Shalaka Prashnavali from Shri Ramcharitmanas, shown with its meaning and the lesson it carries. Choose Hanuman Ji and it is one of the 49 verses of the Hanuman Prashnavali, with its message. Each is drawn for you when you ask, stays the same until midnight, and can be drawn afresh tomorrow. Many devotees read it in the morning as a thought to carry through the day, the way a page-a-day calendar gives a verse.

It is not a horoscope. Nothing about your birth, your sign or the planets goes into it. It is the same practice as a prashnavali, a verse from a sacred text taken as guidance for reflection, with one difference: a prashnavali answers a question you bring, while Today's Guidance is about the day itself.

## How to Read It

Each reading carries one of three tones. A favourable day means the verse is one of promise: begin, continue, expect the good. A day for patience means the verse counsels waiting, or seeking advice before acting. A day for caution means the verse warns of an obstacle, or of a step better not taken today. The tone is a reading of the verse, not a prediction of events.

- Read the verse itself, not only the tone. The chaupai or the Hanuman verse is the guidance; the English line beneath it is a help to the verse.
- One reading from each deity a day. Shri Ram's and Hanuman Ji's readings are drawn separately, and neither changes before midnight.
- Keep your own judgment. A favourable reading is no reason to drop care, and a cautious one is no reason to cancel what must be done.
- Your readings and your count of consecutive days are kept in this browser only, so the count continues as long as you return each day on the same device.
